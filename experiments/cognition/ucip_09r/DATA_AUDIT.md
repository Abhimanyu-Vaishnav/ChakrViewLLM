## 2. Root Cause of Generalization Failure

The mathematical training objective (`shift_logits` and `shift_targets`) is 100% correct. However, the model failed on owner testing because of severe **template entrainment and synthetic suffix memorization**:

1. **Suffix-Conditioned Memorization:**
   In `build_clean_dataset_3600.py`, 50 to 70 variations of only ~50 base concepts were generated by appending synthetic suffixes:
   `User: What is the primary role of a central processing unit (CPU)? (domain query 31)\nAssistant:`
   During training, the model learned to attend to the suffix `(domain query N)` to predict the target. When the owner tested with the natural bare question:
   `User: What does a CPU do?\nAssistant:`
   the model lacked the synthetic suffix trigger, suffered an attention sink failure, and drifted into degenerate subword loops (`"ary ary ary ary..."`).

2. **Absence of Canonical Math Notation:**
   All 600 arithmetic training examples were word problems (`"If you have A apples and receive B more..."`). Not a single training record contained simple mathematical queries like `"What is 2 + 2?"` or `"5 - 2 = ?"`. Consequently, when prompted with `"What is 2 + 2?"`, the model hallucinated `"2 + 2 = 3."`.

3. **High Target Duplication (81.7%):**
   Only 593 unique targets existed across 3,240 examples. The model acted as an associative lookup for 50 fixed response templates rather than learning compositional autoregressive grammar.
