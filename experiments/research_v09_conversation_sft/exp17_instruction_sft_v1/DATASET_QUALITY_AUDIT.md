- **Near-Duplicate Prompt Templates**:

  - *Critical Finding*: This is expected in template-generated benchmarks, but string metrics do not measure conceptual independence. The splits are conceptually isolated via non-overlapping domain entities, numerical offsets, and disjoint mission cycles.

## 7. Diversity & Potential Shortcut Risks

### 7.1 Template Repetition Finding
Auditing unique responses in the Train split (320 records) revealed:
- `factual_qa`: 10 unique responses (4 repetitions each)
- `short_explanation`: 5 unique responses (8 repetitions each)
- `uncertainty_abstention`: 3 unique responses (~13-14 repetitions each)
- `instruction_following`, `context_extraction`, `reference_resolution`, `basic_arithmetic`, `structured_formatting`: 40 unique responses (0 repetitions each).

### 7.2 Potential Shortcut Risks
- **Abstention Collapse Risk**: With only 3 unique abstention strings repeating across 40 Train examples, the model could easily overfit to memorizing these exact phrases rather than learning conditional abstention logic.
- **Explanation Memorization**: In `short_explanation`, repeating the 5 scientific facts 8 times each risks superficial weight-memorization rather than general conversational adherence.

> [!TIP]
> **Recommended Mitigation for Future Iterations**: Before scaling beyond 400 examples or in subsequent v2 iterations, expand the question/fact bank from 5–10 items to 40 distinct items per category to reduce template repetition.
