# Enterprise Billing Terms & Service Level Agreement

## 1. Subscription Tiers
ChakrView Enterprise is billed per active compute node.
- Starter Tier: $150 per month, includes up to 2 concurrent worker processes.
- Professional Tier: $450 per month, includes up to 8 concurrent worker processes and priority telemetry.
- Dedicated Cluster: Custom annual contract starting at $12,000 per year.

## 2. Invoicing and Payment Terms
Invoices are generated on the 1st day of each calendar month.
Payment terms are strictly Net 30 days from invoice issuance date.
Late payments incur an annualized interest surcharge of 8.5% compounded monthly.

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Availability Target: 99.95% uptime for hosted orchestration control planes.
- Downtime Compensation: If monthly uptime drops below 99.9%, a 15% billing credit is applied to the subsequent invoice.
- Maximum Support Response Time: P1 severity incidents require engineer response within 15 minutes.
